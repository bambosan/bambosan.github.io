Version 3.1.0 for MCBE 26.50 to 26.52

About

Mirai is a heavily modified vibrant visual pipeline. It rebuilds 54 materials for the Render Dragon engine, aiming for realism and an atmospheric feel.

It's also a reboot of my earlier shader of the same name, reimagined around a realistic look instead of the old style.

Features

Better reflections
Cleaner screen-space reflections with more vertical tracing.
Atmospheric scattering
A semi-physical sky for natural look.
Better Water caustics
Light patterns projected onto the seabed.
AgX tonemapping
Smoother handling of bright lights and colors.
Cirrus clouds
High-altitude planar cloud layers.
Better Snell's window
A clear view of everything above the water line.
Rain puddle
Puddles that form when it rains make the surface reflective.
Water parallax
An effect that makes the waves on the water's surface appear deeper.

3.0.0


  • Support MCBE 26.50 and above
  • Support for iOS
  • Added simple filtering to screen space reflections
  • Added parallax effect to water waves
  • Added rain puddles on upward-facing surfaces
  • Added rain contribution to sky and atmosphere
  • Added simple distance/boder fog
  • Added new materials

Changed

  • Codebase rebuilt for the new compiler, with a new shader and file structure
  • Improve cirrus cloud shape
  • Improve tonemapping
  • Simplify exposure, now always driven by the scene’s average brightness
  • Reduce water scattering brightness
  • Rework atmospheric scattering
  • Simplify volumetric fog and air scattering
  • Simplify sky probe shading

Removed

  • Remove volumetric clouds, including it's shadows, because the buffer that stored their noise textures is no longer accessible to one of the materials (cirrus clouds remain)
  • Remove the old deferred multipass and tiled materials
  • Remove distant transition fading to subsurface scattering material, now at a distance of about 50 blocks from the player's position, all blocks that have subsurface scattering will looks ugly
  • Remove screen space reflection to the water surface when the camera is in the water

Known Bug

  • Some objects rendered as “Actors” appear completely black (does not occur on Android devices with Mali GPUs)
  • Severe color banding on volumetric fog (Mojang moved the temporal accumulation for froxels but didn’t apply it to the stage where scattering and color are calculated)

Limitation

  • Objects that use forward rendering (e.g. glass, ice and banners) look distracting when viewed from underwater. This is inherent to deferred rendering, not a bug

Yes.
